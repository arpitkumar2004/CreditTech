# Table 2: Multi-Stakeholder & Institutional Mapping

| Stakeholder Category | Specific Institutions / Actors | Role, Mandate & Interest in CreditTech | Regulatory Standing & Legal Interlock |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **End Borrowers** | • Small &amp; marginal farmers (&lt; 2 ha)<br>• Allied producers (dairy, fisheries, poultry)<br>• SHG women entrepreneurs<br>• Rural nano-enterprises (kirana, repair shops) | **Primary Beneficiaries:** Need timely crop credit within 14-day sowing window, lower collateral requirements, fair interest rates (7% KCC vs 36–48% moneylender), and transparent reasons for decisions. | Protected data principals under DPDP Act 2023; statutory right to dispute score and receive plain-language explanation. |
| **Community Institutions** | • Self-Help Groups (SHGs) &amp; Federations (VOs/CLFs)<br>• Farmer Producer Organisations (10,000 FPOs onboarded under SFAC)<br>• Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) | **Trust Anchors &amp; Aggregators:** Provide authentic non-bureau repayment trails (e-Shakti ledgers), peer guarantee confirmation, and bulk farmer aggregation. | Consent granted via group resolutions + individual opt-in; acts as field validation partner. |
| **Regulated Lenders (REs)** | • Regional Rural Banks (RRBs — 43 banks)<br>• Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS — 63k+)<br>• Small Finance Banks (SFBs)<br>• NBFC-MFIs &amp; Commercial Banks | **Capital &amp; Risk Owners:** Consume alternative credit score for underwriting; hold the loan assets on balance sheet; make ultimate sanction and disbursal decisions. | Strictly regulated entities under RBI Banking Regulation Act; governed by RBI Digital Lending Directions (2022/2026). |
| **Apex &amp; Development Bodies** | • NABARD (Agri &amp; Rural Development)<br>• SIDBI (MSME Development)<br>• SFAC (Small Farmers' Agri-Business Consortium)<br>• National Cooperative Development Corp (NCDC) | **Policy &amp; Refinance Anchors:** Provide concessional refinance, pilot grant sponsorships, FPO support, and rural financial inclusion monitoring. | National statutory development banks; set priority sector lending (PSL) guidelines with RBI. |
| **Regulators &amp; Policymakers** | • Reserve Bank of India (DoR, FinTech Dept)<br>• Data Protection Board of India (MeitY)<br>• Ministry of Agriculture &amp; Farmers Welfare<br>• Ministry of Rural Development (DAY-NRLM) | **Regulatory Guardrails:** Enforce lending conduct, Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) 5% caps, data privacy, fair practices, and priority lending targets. | Issuers of statutory directions (RBI DLG 2022/2026, DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025). |
| **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)** | • Sahamati (Account Aggregator Ecosystem)<br>• RBI Innovation Hub (Unified Lending Interface)<br>• NPCI (UPI, AePS, e-NACH)<br>• UIDAI &amp; DigiLocker (Identity) | **National Data &amp; Transaction Rails:** Enable frictionless consent-based pulling of bank statements, state land records, and identity tokens. | Regulated public and non-profit digital utilities; certification required for FIU/LSP access. |
| **Alternative Data Partners** | • Satellite/EO providers (Sentinel-2, SatSure)<br>• Mandi &amp; Value Chain platforms (e-NAM)<br>• Agri-input retailers &amp; dairy cooperatives | **Signal Suppliers:** Provide 10m multispectral NDVI imagery, historical yield estimates, mandi sales transactions, and daily milk pouring data. | Commercial API contracts; subject to DPDP purpose limitation and data licensing agreements. |
| **Civil Society &amp; Academic Auditors** | • Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs)<br>• Bank Sakhi &amp; Business Correspondent Networks<br>• Academic Evaluation Teams (IIT Kharagpur) | **Field Enablement &amp; Model Governance:** Conduct assisted farmer onboarding, digital literacy camps, and independent algorithmic fairness audits (DIR ≥ 0.80). | Independent research and third-party algorithmic audit role; zero commercial interest in loan portfolio. |
