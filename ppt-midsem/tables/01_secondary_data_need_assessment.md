# Table 1: Secondary Data Benchmarks & Rural Credit Need Assessment

| Secondary Data Source / Benchmark | Published Metric / Finding | Structural Implication for CreditTech |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **World Bank Global Findex (2025 / 2024)** | • **89%** of Indian adults hold a bank account (up from 35% in 2011).<br>• Only **13%** of adults borrowed formally in the prior year (vs 23% developing economy average).<br>• Only **10%** of rural women borrowed formally. | **The Inclusion Paradox:** India has solved bank account ownership, but not credit access. Formal banks do not lend without credit history. |
| **RBI Financial Inclusion Index (FI-Index, July 2025)** | • FI-Index reached **67.0** (0–100 scale).<br>• **Usage** dimension (weighted 45%) substantially lags **Access** (weighted 35%).<br>• Payments drive usage; credit remains deeply underserved. | Digital payments (UPI) generate transaction trails that can be converted into alternative creditworthiness signals via Account Aggregator. |
| **NABARD All-India Rural Financial Inclusion Survey (NAFIS 2021–22)** | • **57%** of rural households are agricultural (up from 48% in 2016–17).<br>• Average landholding has shrunk to **0.74 hectares** (down from 1.08 ha in 2016–17).<br>• **86%** of farmers are small and marginal (&lt; 2 ha).<br>• **23.4%** of agricultural households borrow exclusively from high-cost informal moneylenders. | Traditional land-mortgage underwriting fails 86% of farmers. Need cashflow-based and agronomic alternative scoring rather than collateral. |
| **SIDBI–CRISIL MSME Report (May 2025)** | • Addressable MSME credit gap of **₹30 Lakh Crore** (24% of total debt demand).<br>• Credit gap widens to **35%** for women-owned rural micro-enterprises.<br>• **12%** of micro-enterprises still borrow informally. | Rural micro-enterprises lack audited P&amp;L and GST filings; requires alternative scoring based on UPI merchant trails and SHG thrift history. |
| **RBI Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India (2023–24)** | • Agriculture sector Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) at **6.2%** vs **2.5%** system-wide.<br>• Retail loans have lowest GNPA (1.2%). | Commercial banks are structurally risk-averse toward agriculture due to high NPA perception. Objective multi-source scoring de-risks rural portfolios. |
| **DAY-NRLM / PIB Rural Development (Dec 2025)** | • **10.05 Crore** rural women mobilised into **90.9 Lakh** Self-Help Groups.<br>• Cumulative bank credit disbursed exceeds **₹12.18 Lakh Crore**.<br>• Sustained repayment rate **&gt; 96%**. | Massive community social capital and repayment discipline exists, but is completely invisible to CIBIL/Experian credit bureaus. |
| **IAMAI–Kantar Internet in India Report (2025)** | • **548 Million** active internet users in rural India (out of 958M nationally).<br>• Rural internet user growth is **4x faster** than urban.<br>• 98% consume content in Indic regional languages. | Rural connectivity floor is now sufficient for smartphone PWA apps, assisted Bank Sakhi tablet onboarding, and mobile consent flows. |
