# Table 3: Comparative Analysis — Existing System (As-Is) vs. Proposed System (To-Be)

| Evaluation Dimension | Existing System (As-Is: Traditional Rural Lending) | Proposed System (To-Be: CreditTech Alternative Scoring) | Engineering & Institutional Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Underwriting Inputs** | • Past credit bureau repayment history (CIBIL/Experian)<br>• Registered land mortgage / physical patta deed<br>• Physical field verification by loan officer<br>• Third-party guarantor with institutional standing | • Multi-rail alternative data: Account Aggregator banking cashflow trails<br>• Sentinel-2 10m satellite NDVI crop vigor &amp; drought risk<br>• SHG e-Shakti thrift ledgers &amp; FPO mandi sale receipts<br>• Bureau pull only where it exists (corroborating, not gating) | Replaces backward-looking debt history with forward-looking economic activity and community trust signals. |
| **Turnaround Time (TAT)** | • **10 to 20 business days** for KCC/agri loan appraisal.<br>• Multiple in-person branch visits (average 3–5 trips). | • **&lt; 3 minutes** for end-to-end data pull and score computation.<br>• **Same-day** digital sanction by partner Regulated Entity. | Farmers face rigid 14-day Kharif sowing windows; long TAT forces them into exploitative 36–48% moneylender debt. |
| **New-to-Credit (Thin-File) Treatment** | • Automatically excluded or scored as **"-1"** (No History).<br>• Borrowers without formal salary slips or ITR rejected at branch gate. | • Scoreable on a standardized **300 to 900 scale** using 21 domain features across the 5 Cs of credit (Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, Conditions). | Enables credit access for 120M+ unbanked smallholders and women SHG members with zero prior bureau files. |
| **Decision Transparency &amp; Explainability** | • Opaque manual discretion; rejection reasons rarely communicated to applicant.<br>• High vulnerability to local patronage and unconscious human bias. | • **Exact closed-form bilingual SHAP reasons** (Hindi &amp; English).<br>• Top-5 score drivers delivered to both farmer and loan officer.<br>• Mandatory audit log for any underwriter override dissent. | Complies with RBI digital lending disclosure norms and Fair Practices Code; provides actionable financial recourse to borrowers. |
| **Institutional &amp; Regulatory Alignment** | • Bank / PACS operates monolithic in-house appraisal.<br>• High fixed operating costs for small-ticket loans (₹25k–₹50k). | • **Unbundled LSP / TSP model** under RBI Digital Lending Guidelines.<br>• CreditTech provides intelligence; partner bank holds credit risk and loan capital.<br>• ULI and OCEN interoperable. | Drastically reduces bank's cost-to-serve while ensuring CreditTech holds zero balance-sheet default risk. |
| **System Resilience &amp; Partial Data** | • Hard failure: If physical land deed is missing or illegible, entire application halts. | • **Graceful Degradation:** Rescales feature weights and widens confidence interval (e.g. $720 \pm 45$ vs $\pm 15$) if an external rail times out. | Rural connectivity and government land digitisation are uneven; graceful degradation prevents systemic borrower lock-out. |
